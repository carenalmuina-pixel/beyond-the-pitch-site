The Start Your Business
Checklist.

The honest first steps to starting your business — in order, with the boring-but-critical parts most guides skip left in.

1Find the real problemFoundation
  • Name the person, the problem, and the result in one sentence — before touching colors or a logo.
  • Ask 5 real people how they handle this problem today. Don't pitch — just listen.
  • Write down who this is for, in their own words — not an invented "ideal customer."
2Shape the first offerFoundation
  • Choose the one thing you'll sell first. Everything else is version two.
  • Decide: service (trades your time) or product (works without you) — build accordingly.
  • Price it for what it's worth to the customer, not what feels safe to charge.
3Make it officialOften skipped
  • Pick a name that's easy to say, easy to spell, and won't box you in.
  • Open a separate business bank account before your first sale.
  • Confirm what's legally required to launch — licensing, terms, privacy policy.
4Build the minimumFoundation
  • Build a minimum viable brand: one name, one color, one font. Upgrade later.
  • Set a 90-day plan in three blocks — Build, Test, Launch — with one clear checkpoint each.
  • Know your 4 numbers: what you made, what it cost, what's left, how long that lasts.
5Get your first customersFoundation
  • Treat your first 10 customers as research, not just revenue — watch what they do, not just what they say.
  • Review what you learned and adjust the offer before you scale anything.
Before you go further

This is general educational information, not legal, tax, or financial advice. Verify what applies to your specific business and jurisdiction with a licensed professional before you file, sign, or spend.
