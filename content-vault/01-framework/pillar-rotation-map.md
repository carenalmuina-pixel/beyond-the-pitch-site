# 90-Day / 7-Pillar Rotation Map

The full production sequence, one row per day (each day carries 3 scripts:
AM-LEARN, MIDDAY-DO, PM-CONNECT — same topic, three jobs). Source of truth:
`../00-source/beyond-the-pitch-90-day-270-script-content-calendar.xlsx`.

Pillars rotate on a fixed 7-day cycle — Foundation, Brand, Marketing, Sales,
Systems, Money, Leadership & Founder, repeat — so the account reads as a
complete coaching platform from day one instead of 13 days of one topic
before moving to the next. Foundation and Brand run 13 rotations (91 would be
day 92 and 99, past the 90-day window, so both land at 13); Leadership &
Founder runs 12.
