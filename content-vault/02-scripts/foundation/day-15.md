# Day 15 — Choose a simple first offer

Pillar: Foundation · Offer/Folder: Foundation First · Week 3 · Rotation: 3 of 13

## Shared production notes

- **Motion spec (all 3 posts):** 9:16 master, 1080×1920. 3–5 stills per post.
  Slow push-in 103–108%, gentle pan, 0.3–0.5s cross-dissolve, minimal text
  motion, captions centered in the safe zone. BP submark small and
  consistent. No flashy transitions.
- **Shared B-roll pool for the day:** a whiteboard or notebook with a long
  list of ideas being narrowed down, one item circled, a hand crossing out
  extra features, a simple one-line offer written large.
- **Visual base (all 3 posts):** Beyond the Pitch brand aesthetic — warm
  desk, whiteboard or large notebook, laptop, soft window light; ivory
  `#FBF7F2` base, moss `#3E4432`, sage `#6F7154`, terracotta `#D08B6E`
  accents; warm editorial natural light; subtle paper/brush texture; clean
  negative space; no generic corporate stock look.

## AM — LEARN — Choose a simple first offer

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> [CAREN: name the venture and the moment the offer list got too long —
> were you trying to launch five things instead of one?] I had a list of
> everything the product could eventually do, and I was about to build all
> of it at once. A first offer only needs to do one thing well enough that
> someone pays for it. Everything else is version two. [CAREN: show the one
> thing you circled — the actual first offer you landed on] The goal isn't
> to make it look more complete. It's to make it easy to say yes to.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* circling "one export preset,
done well" on a whiteboard that had twelve other OmniClip feature ideas
crossed out around it.

**On-screen text:**
```
Choose a simple first offer.
1 idea. 1 clear takeaway.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Wide shot of a whiteboard covered in ideas.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Hand starting to cross items out, one by one.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Landing on and circling the one that remains.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Close-up of the single circled offer, clean and legible.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — BP submark end card, CTA text.

## MIDDAY — DO — Choose a simple first offer

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Assignment: list everything your offer could eventually include, then
> cross out everything except the one thing someone would pay for today.
> [CAREN: do this live for whichever venture needs it most right now] Don't
> keep the "nice to have" version to feel safer — the shorter list is the
> harder, more useful one. Write the one-line offer that's left. Save this,
> do it, and use that one line the next time someone asks what you're
> building.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* narrowing Klypt's onboarding flow
down to one screen instead of the five she'd sketched.

**On-screen text:**
```
DO THIS TODAY
Cross out everything but the one thing.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Text card: "Your 10-minute assignment" over a notebook list.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — A long list visible, pen hovering, reluctance to cut.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Crossing out item after item, decisively.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — The one remaining line, written large and clear.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — End card: "Save this. Do it today."

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Overhead shot of a notebook page with a long handwritten list, pen hovering above it, warm natural light, editorial photography, no readable text."
2. "Close-up of a hand crossing out multiple lines in a notebook with terracotta ink, warm window light, shallow depth of field."
3. "Close-up of one remaining handwritten line on an otherwise crossed-out page, bold and clear, warm natural light, clean composition."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; hold 4–5s on the final remaining-line still since it's the deliverable.

**B-roll:** Pen hovering with reluctance; a full page being turned to reveal the one clean sentence.

**Caption:**
> List everything your offer could include. Cross out everything except the
> one thing someone would pay for today. The shorter list is the harder,
> more useful one. Save this, do it, use that one line. #BeyondThePitch
> #FoundationFirst #SmallBusiness

**Platform variations:**

## PM — CONNECT — Choose a simple first offer

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Honest moment: [CAREN: what did it feel like to cut the list down — did
> it feel like giving something up, or like relief?] Cutting the list felt
> like admitting I couldn't do everything at once. It wasn't relief right
> away — it was closer to loss. But a business that tries to be everything
> on day one usually becomes nothing you can actually finish. Beyond the
> Pitch isn't about having it all figured out. It's about making the cut
> anyway. If you're staring at your own long list tonight, save this.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* sitting with the crossed-out list
for an evening before feeling okay about what got cut from the public-
adjusting business's first service package.

**On-screen text:**
```
DIFFERENT FIELD. SAME DRIVE.
Cutting the list isn't relief. It's the job anyway.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Founder shot, looking at the crossed-out list quietly.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — A moment of stillness, not immediately relieved.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Voiceover over a calmer visual, path/window/horizon.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Setting the list aside, moving forward with the one line.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — "Different field. Same drive." end card.

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Editorial photo of a woman sitting quietly at a desk looking at a crossed-out list, contemplative expression, warm window light, Beyond the Pitch palette, no text overlay."
2. "Wide shot of a wooden path through golden hills at sunset, warm tones, symbolic of moving forward, editorial photography, no people, no text."
3. "Editorial photo of a woman setting a notebook aside with quiet resolve, hand still on the page, warm natural light, clean composition."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec, slowest push-in (102%→106%) of the three posts; extend the stillness beat before any pan begins.

**B-roll:** A long exhale; hand resting flat on a closed notebook; the walk from desk to window.

**Caption:**
> Cutting the list felt like admitting I couldn't do everything at once. Not
> relief — closer to loss. But trying to be everything on day one usually

> you're staring at your own long list tonight, save this. #BeyondThePitch
