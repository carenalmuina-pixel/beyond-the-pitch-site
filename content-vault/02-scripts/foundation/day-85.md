## MIDDAY — DO — Your first 10 customers are research

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Assignment: list your first ten customers (or your first ten serious
> prospective ones if you're pre-revenue), and next to each, write one
> thing you noticed about how they actually used or reacted to what you
> offered. [CAREN: do this live for whichever venture has enough real
> contact to work with] Look for the pattern that repeats at least three
> times — that's your signal. Save this, build the list, and circle the
> repeat.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* going through Curblist's first
ten beta conversations and noticing the same friction point mentioned by
four different people, which she'd been treating as one-off complaints.

**On-screen text:**
```
DO THIS TODAY
List 10. Note what they did. Circle the repeat.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Text card: "Your 10-minute assignment" over a blank numbered list.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Filling in the list, unsure what to note beyond names.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Adding one honest observation per entry.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Circling the pattern that appears three or more times.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — End card: "Save this. Do it today."

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Overhead shot of a blank numbered list on a notebook page, warm natural light, editorial photography, abstracted handwriting."
2. "Close-up of a hand writing a short observation beside a numbered entry, warm window light, shallow depth of field."
3. "Overhead shot of a notebook page with a repeated pattern circled across multiple entries, terracotta ink, warm natural light, clean composition."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; no unusual variation, procedural pacing matching the list-building exercise.

**B-roll:** A hand tallying repeat mentions; stepping back to view the full list.

**Caption:**
> List your first 10 customers (or prospective ones). Next to each, write
> one thing you noticed about how they actually reacted. Circle whatever
> repeats at least three times — that's your signal. Save this, build the
> list today. #BeyondThePitch #FoundationFirst #SmallBusiness
