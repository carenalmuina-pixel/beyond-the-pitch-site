**Full script (25–40 sec):**

> before the product was proven] I was three logo concepts deep before a
> single person had used the product. A minimum viable brand is a name, one
> color, one font, and a plain wordmark — enough to look intentional, not
> enough to have wasted a month on. [CAREN: show the actual MVB you landed

## MIDDAY — DO — Create a minimum viable brand

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Assignment: pick one color, one font, and write your business name in
> plain text — that's your MVB. [CAREN: do this live for whichever venture
> is over-designed or under-designed right now] If you're already past
> this, audit whether you spent more time on brand than the business has
> earned yet. Fix the mismatch, not by undoing the brand, but by getting the
> product moving to catch up. Save this, pick your three, and stop there for
> now.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* picking terracotta, Lato, and
"Lumina" typed plain — done in four minutes instead of the four hours she'd
budgeted for a "quick" brand pass.

**On-screen text:**
```
DO THIS TODAY
1 color. 1 font. Plain text name. Stop there.
```

**Storyboard:**

3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Choosing one color, one font, typing the name plainly.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — The finished minimum viable brand, deliberately simple.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — End card: "Save this. Do it today."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; no unusual variation, deliberately unhurried to model the "stop there" restraint.

**B-roll:** Extra swatches set aside; a hand closing the laptop after finishing quickly.

## PM — CONNECT — Create a minimum viable brand

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Honestly: [CAREN: what did stopping at "plain" feel like — did it feel
> unfinished, embarrassing, freeing?] Shipping something that looked plain
> felt like admitting it wasn't ready to be beautiful yet. I wanted it to
> look like it mattered before I'd proven that it did. Beyond the Pitch is
> choosing plain and honest over polished and premature, on camera, even
> when it feels like less. If you're sitting on an over-designed idea that
> hasn't shipped, save this.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* the specific discomfort of
posting Klypt's plain, unstyled first screenshots publicly instead of
waiting for the "real" design pass.

**On-screen text:**
```
DIFFERENT FIELD. SAME DRIVE.
Plain and honest beats polished and premature.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Founder shot, hovering over a "post" button with a plain screenshot.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Hesitation — wanting to wait for it to look more finished.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Voiceover over a calmer visual — path, window, horizon.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Posting it anyway, plain and unpolished.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — "Different field. Same drive." end card.

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Editorial photo of a woman's hand hovering over a phone screen about to post something, hesitant posture, warm window light, Beyond the Pitch palette, no text overlay."
2. "Wide shot of a wooden path through golden hills at sunset, warm tones, symbolic of choosing honesty over polish, editorial photography, no people, no text."
3. "Editorial photo of a woman setting the phone down with quiet resolve after posting, warm natural light, clean composition."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec, slowest push-in (102%→106%); extend the hesitation beat before the post happens.

**B-roll:** Finger hovering over a "post" button; a slow exhale after tapping.

**Caption:**
> Shipping something plain felt like admitting it wasn't ready to be
> beautiful yet. I wanted it to look like it mattered before I'd proven
> that it did. Choosing plain and honest over polished and premature, on
> camera. If you're sitting on something over-designed and unshipped, save
> this. #BeyondThePitch #FounderReality #BuildInPublic
