## PM — CONNECT — Define your ideal customer without inventing a fantasy person

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Honest moment: [CAREN: what did it feel like to realize you'd invented a
> customer instead of listening to a real one — was there a specific
> discomfort in that?] Inventing the persona was easier because a made-up
> person always likes what you're building. A real one might not. That's
> the actual risk — not the worksheet, the listening. Beyond the Pitch means
> choosing the real conversation even when it might tell me something I
> don't want to hear. If you've been designing for a person who doesn't
> exist, save this.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* the sting of a real Lumina
prospective user saying plainly that a feature she loved wasn't the reason
they'd pay, and sitting with that instead of dismissing it.

**On-screen text:**
```
DIFFERENT FIELD. SAME DRIVE.
A made-up customer always likes what you're building. A real one might not.
```
