# Brand System Reference

This is the brand already live on the site — pulled from `../../index.html`'s
CSS variables and the Brand System sheet in the source xlsx. Nothing here is
new or proposed; it's the condensed version to check against while writing or
designing a script, so you don't have to open the CSS or the spreadsheet.

## Brand promise & tagline

- **Promise:** Real guidance. Real results. Real freedom.
- **Tagline:** Hype, into the real work. — use sparingly, as a signature line
  or end-card device, not on every asset.

## Typography

- **Heading:** Cormorant Garamond — large editorial headlines only.
- **Body:** Lato — captions, subheads, instructional text, on-screen body copy.
- **Accent script:** Playlist Script (site fallback: Beau Rivage, a free
  Google Font with the same flowing calligraphic character) — 1–4 key words
  max, never body copy, never a full sentence.

## Voice

Empowering, strategic, supportive, elegant, growth-focused. Clear, grounded,
useful, warm. Never hype-heavy, never condescending, never "buy my course to
find out." The brand's own words for what it isn't: *"We don't do hype. We
build real businesses and real freedom."*

For documentary posts specifically: the voice stays the same, but the proof
now comes from showing real work instead of stating a claim. "Here's the
decision I made and why" reads as this brand; "10 things every founder needs
to know" doesn't.

## Imagery & mood

Warm editorial, natural light, paper texture, organic workspaces, paths and
horizons. Avoid glossy corporate stock, neon tech, heavy gradients, staged
"hustle" imagery. Recurring elements: the BP monogram, the arrow/swoosh, a
heart, leaves, paths, subtle brush textures — use 1–3 per asset, never clutter
the frame with all of them.

## Logo usage

Use the primary logo (`bp` mark + "beyond the pitch" wordmark + "Hype, into
the real work." tagline) for most applications. Use the submark/icon-only
version — moss, terracotta, or ivory circle with the `bp` monogram — for
social avatars, favicons, watermarks, and small-space end cards. The site's
working files are in `../../assets/` (`logo-mark.png`, `logo-primary.png`,
`logo-badge-terracotta.png`).
