## AM — LEARN — Validate before you build

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> [CAREN: name the venture and the build decision you almost made without
> checking it against a real person first] I almost built that before
> asking anyone if they wanted it. Validation isn't a survey — it's finding
> out whether a real person has the problem badly enough to already be doing
> something about it. [CAREN: what did you actually do to check — texted a
> real user, asked in a community, looked at what people already pay for?]
> Building without that is expensive guessing dressed up as progress. Ask
> first. Build second.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* "I almost built a whole scheduling
feature into Curblist before I asked five actual adjusters if they even
wanted it inside the app instead of their existing calendar."

**On-screen text:**
```
Validate before you build.
1 idea. 1 clear takeaway.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Hands mid-type on a feature spec, pause.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Cut to the moment of doubt — "does anyone actually want this?"
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Sending a real message to a real prospective user, waiting.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Reply comes in on screen — real feedback, unedited.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — BP submark end card, CTA text.

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Close-up of hands typing on a laptop with a feature spec document open, pause mid-motion, warm desk setting, editorial photography, Beyond the Pitch palette, no visible screen text."

3. "Close-up of a phone in hand with a notification appearing, warm editorial light, terracotta phone case or desk accent, clean composition."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; slight zoom-punch on the reply notification still to emphasize the payoff moment.

**B-roll:** Hand typing a message; phone resting screen-up on the desk while waiting for a reply.

**Caption:**
> Validation isn't a survey — it's finding out whether a real person has the
> problem badly enough to already be doing something about it. Ask first.
> Build second. Following the real build across the portfolio — including

## MIDDAY — DO — Validate before you build

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Today's assignment: message five real people who might have this problem
> — not your friends, actual prospective users — and ask one question: "how
> are you dealing with [problem] right now?" [CAREN: show the actual
> messages you sent, blurred if needed for privacy] Don't pitch anything.
> Just listen for what they're already doing. If nobody's doing anything
> about it, that's data too — just not the data you wanted. Send the
> messages, save this, and come back with what you heard.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* messaging three people in a
Lumina-adjacent Facebook group asking how they currently track something,
screenshots blurred for privacy.

**On-screen text:**
```
DO THIS TODAY
Message 5 real people. Ask, don't pitch.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Text card: "Your 10-minute assignment" over a phone.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Blank message box, cursor blinking, hesitation.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Typing and sending the question, one after another.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — A tally mark or checklist showing 5 sent.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — End card: "Save this. Do it today."

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Close-up of a phone with an empty message compose box, cursor blinking, warm natural light, shallow depth of field, no readable UI text."
2. "Overhead shot of a hand typing on a phone, warm desk in the background with a notebook and coffee, editorial photography, Beyond the Pitch palette."
3. "Flat-lay of a notebook with a hand-drawn tally of five checkmarks, terracotta ink, warm natural light, clean negative space."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; slightly faster pacing on the "sending" scene (rapid-fire feel) to match the assignment's momentum.

**B-roll:** Sticky note wall with early feedback quotes; a printed screenshot pinned to a corkboard.

**Caption:**
> Message 5 real people who might have this problem. Ask "how are you
> dealing with this right now?" Don't pitch — just listen. No response is
> data too. Save this, do it today, come back with what you heard.
> #BeyondThePitch #FoundationFirst #ValidateBeforeYouBuild

**Platform variations:**
- **IG/FB Reel:** As scripted.
- **TikTok:** Lead with the exact question to ask as bold on-screen text before any footage — this is the most save-worthy line.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Keep the "ask, don't pitch" framing prominent throughout, not just in the hook.
- **Pinterest Idea Pin:** Turn into a swipeable "validation script" — the exact message template to copy.
- **Blog expansion angle:** "The exact message I send before building anything" — publish the real template as a resource.

## PM — CONNECT — Validate before you build

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> The part nobody posts: [CAREN: what did it feel like to ask and wait for
> an answer — was there a version where you were scared of what they'd say,
> or scared no one would answer at all?] Asking feels riskier than building,
> because building alone lets you stay in control of the story. The second
> you ask a real person, the story becomes real — and it might not be the
> one you wanted. That's the trade every founder makes eventually. Beyond
> the Pitch is about making that trade on purpose, not by accident. If
> you're mid-ask right now, save this.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* the two hours between sending the
message and getting a reply, refreshing the app, half-hoping no one would
answer so she wouldn't have to change the plan.

**On-screen text:**
```
DIFFERENT FIELD. SAME DRIVE.
Asking feels riskier than building. That's the trade.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Founder shot, phone face-down on the desk, waiting.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Checking the phone, no reply yet, setting it back down.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Voiceover over a calmer visual — hands on a notebook, steady.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Picking the phone back up, reading a reply, quiet reaction.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — "Different field. Same drive." end card.

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Editorial photo of a woman at a desk with a phone face-down beside her, contemplative expression, warm window light, Beyond the Pitch palette, no text overlay."
2. "Close-up of hands resting on an open notebook, steady and calm, warm natural light, paper texture visible, clean composition."
3. "Editorial photo of a woman reading something on her phone with a quiet, thoughtful reaction, warm desk setting, soft natural light."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; slowest push-in of the three (102%→106%), extra beat of stillness on the "waiting" scene before the pan begins.

**B-roll:** Phone resting screen-down; hands still on a notebook; a slow exhale moment.

**Caption:**
> Asking feels riskier than building, because building alone lets you stay
> in control of the story. The second you ask a real person, it becomes
> real. That's the trade every founder makes eventually. If you're mid-ask
> right now, save this. #BeyondThePitch #FounderReality #BuildInPublic

**Platform variations:**
- **IG/FB Reel:** As scripted, ambient/instrumental audio, slower cut.
- **TikTok:** Open on the concrete waiting moment (phone face-down) rather than the abstract line — concrete first on this platform.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Minimal captions, let the pacing carry it.
- **Pinterest Idea Pin:** Skip — mood-driven founder content underperforms as a static pin.
- **Blog expansion angle:** Fold into the monthly "what building actually looked like" recap rather than a standalone post.
