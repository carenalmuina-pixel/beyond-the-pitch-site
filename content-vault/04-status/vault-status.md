## What "fully vaulted" actually means here

Every Foundation script has real content in every section — but the
documentary specifics are `[CAREN: ...]` placeholders by design, not filled
with invented data. **"Vaulted" means production-ready structure and
evergreen teaching content, not a finished, postable video.** Nobody can
finish these without Caren filling in what's actually true that week. See
`../01-framework/documentary-template.md` for why.
