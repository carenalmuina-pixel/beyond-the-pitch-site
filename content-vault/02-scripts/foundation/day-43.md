## MIDDAY — DO — Understand your basic business numbers

> zero new revenue. [CAREN: do this live for one venture] Don't build a
> dashboard yet. Write them on paper first so you actually see them. This is
> general information to build the habit — not financial advice for your
> specific situation. Save this, find your numbers, and write them down
> today.
