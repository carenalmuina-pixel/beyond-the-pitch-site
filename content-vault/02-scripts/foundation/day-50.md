## MIDDAY — DO — Build a 90-day launch plan

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Assignment: take your next launch and write three headers — Build, Test,
> Launch — across 30 days each. Under each, write the ONE thing that has to
> be true before you move to the next block. [CAREN: do this live for the
> venture that needs it most] Don't fill in every task yet — just the
> gate for each phase. Save this, block out your 90 days, and post the gates
> where you'll see them daily.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* writing "5 real users complete
onboarding without help" as the single gate before Curblist moves from
Build into Test.

**On-screen text:**
```
DO THIS TODAY
3 blocks. 1 gate each. That's the whole plan.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Text card: "Your 10-minute assignment" over a blank three-column layout.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Staring at the blank "Build" column, unsure what the gate is.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Writing the one clear gate for each of the three blocks.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — All three gates written, visible together.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — End card: "Save this. Do it today."

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Overhead shot of a notebook page with three blank column headers, warm natural light, editorial photography, abstracted handwriting."
2. "Close-up of a hand writing a single line under a column header, terracotta ink, warm window light, shallow depth of field."
3. "Overhead shot of a notebook page with three filled-in gate lines under three headers, warm natural light, clean composition."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; no unusual variation — procedural pacing to match the structured assignment.

**B-roll:** Sticky note being placed on a visible wall/board; a hand stepping back to check placement.

**Caption:**
> Write three headers — Build, Test, Launch — 30 days each. Under each,
> write the ONE thing that has to be true before you move on. Don't fill in
> every task yet, just the gate. Save this, block out your 90 days today.
> #BeyondThePitch #FoundationFirst #SmallBusiness

## PM — CONNECT — Build a 90-day launch plan

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Real talk: [CAREN: what was scarier — setting the plan, or the possibility
> of the gate not being met on time?] Writing the plan was easy. Committing
> to the gate — the one honest checkpoint that could tell me it's not ready
> — was harder, because it meant I couldn't just keep building forever to
> avoid finding out. Beyond the Pitch is showing the plan and whether the
> gates actually held. If you're avoiding setting a real checkpoint, save
> this.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* the discomfort of setting a real
test-phase gate for OmniClip, knowing it meant she'd have to actually show
it to strangers on a fixed date instead of polishing indefinitely.
