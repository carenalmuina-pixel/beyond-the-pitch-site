# Day 1 — Start with the problem, not the logo

Pillar: Foundation · Offer/Folder: Foundation First · Week 1 · Rotation: 1 of 13

## Shared production notes

- **Motion spec (all 3 posts):** 9:16 master, 1080×1920. 3–5 stills per post.
  Slow push-in 103–108%, gentle left/right pan, 0.3–0.5s cross-dissolve
  between stills, minimal text motion, captions centered in the safe zone.
  Keep the BP submark small and consistent. No flashy transitions.
- **Shared B-roll pool for the day:** a blank notebook page, a pen resting on
  an unfinished sentence, a laptop cursor blinking in an empty doc, a hand
  closing a laptop, a walk from desk to window and back, coffee going cold
  next to a notepad.
- **Visual base (all 3 posts):** Beyond the Pitch brand aesthetic — warm
  desk, notebook, business checklist, laptop, folders, soft window light;
  ivory `#FBF7F2` base, moss `#3E4432`, sage `#6F7154`, terracotta `#D08B6E`
  accents; warm editorial natural light; subtle paper/brush texture; clean
  negative space; no generic corporate stock look.

## AM — LEARN — Start with the problem, not the logo

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> [CAREN: name the venture and the specific moment — were you about to pick
> colors, a name, a font, before you'd written down who it's for and what
> problem it solves?] That's the moment most people skip past. A business
> gets clearer the second you can name the person, the problem, and the
> result — before you touch colors or a website. The goal isn't to make it
> look more finished. It's to make the next decision easier and the company
> more sustainable. [CAREN: show the actual one-sentence version you wrote —
> "I help ___ solve ___ so they can ___" — even if it's rough.] Foundation
> first. Complexity only when the business earns it.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* "I had OmniClip's export screen
half-designed before I'd written down who actually opens it and why."

**On-screen text:**
```
Start with the problem, not the logo.
1 idea. 1 clear takeaway.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Close on a half-built brand moodboard or logo sketch, hand pauses mid-motion.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Cut to a blank "who is this for?" line in a notebook, unanswered.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Hands writing the one-sentence formula on paper, unhurried.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Camera lands on the finished sentence, underlined once.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — BP submark end card, tagline, CTA text.

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Overhead shot of a half-finished brand moodboard — color swatches and a sketched logo — on a warm wood desk, soft window light, editorial photography, Beyond the Pitch palette (ivory, moss, sage, terracotta), no text overlay."

3. "Overhead shot of a hand writing in a notebook, warm editorial light, sage and terracotta accents in the frame (a terracotta mug, a sage-covered planner), paper texture visible."
4. "Close-up of a finished handwritten sentence in a notebook, underlined in terracotta ink, soft focus background, warm natural light."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec above; hold slightly longer (4–5s) on the Proof still since it carries the payoff.

**B-roll:** Blank notebook page; pen resting on an unfinished line; hand closing a laptop.

**Caption:**
> Start with the problem, not the logo. A business gets clearer the moment

## MIDDAY — DO — Start with the problem, not the logo

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> Here's the assignment: write one sentence. "I help [person] solve
> [problem] so they can [result]." Don't perfect it on the first pass —
> [CAREN: show yourself actually doing this live for one of the ventures,
> crossing out a version that didn't work] make the simplest usable version,
> date it, and come back to it once you have real customer or operating
> data. A small system you actually use beats an elaborate one that never
> gets finished. Do it, save this, and check back once it's done.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* crossing out "I help creators edit
faster" and rewriting it as "I help solo creators cut a week of editing down
to an afternoon."

**On-screen text:**
```
DO THIS TODAY
Start with the problem, not the logo.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Text card: "Your 10-minute assignment" over notebook.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — First attempt at the sentence, crossed out.
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Rewriting it, slower, more deliberate handwriting.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Dated version in the corner of the page, circled.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — End card: "Save this. Do it today."

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Flat-lay of an open notebook with a crossed-out sentence, pen and a terracotta-accented planner beside it, warm natural light, editorial photography."
2. "Close-up of a hand rewriting a sentence in a notebook, warm window light, shallow depth of field, paper texture visible."
3. "Overhead shot of a notebook page with a dated, circled sentence in the corner, terracotta ink accent, clean negative space, Beyond the Pitch palette."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; quick cut (2s) on the crossed-out first attempt to keep energy up before slowing for the rewrite.

**B-roll:** Coffee going cold next to the notepad; cursor blinking in an empty doc (for anyone doing this digitally instead of by hand).

**Caption:**
> Write one sentence: I help [person] solve [problem] so they can [result].
> Don't perfect it — date it, use it, revisit it once you have real data.
> Save this and come back when it's done. #BeyondThePitch #FoundationFirst
> #SmallBusiness

**Platform variations:**
- **IG/FB Reel:** As scripted.
- **TikTok:** Open on the crossed-out version first (pattern interrupt), then reveal the assignment.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Pin the formula as on-screen text for the full duration, not just one scene.
- **Pinterest Idea Pin:** Turn into a 3-slide carousel: the formula, an example, a blank template to save.
- **Blog expansion angle:** Turn the assignment into a downloadable one-page worksheet, linked from Resources.

## PM — CONNECT — Start with the problem, not the logo

**Full script (25–40 sec):**
> A reminder for the founder who feels behind: [CAREN: name the real moment
> this week you caught yourself hiding in planning instead of deciding —
> which venture, what were you avoiding?] Most people hide inside planning
> because choosing a real problem makes the idea feel real — and real means
> it can be wrong. Clarity comes from deciding, not decorating. This is what
> Beyond the Pitch actually is: not performance, the decisions, habits, and
> conversations that build something real. You're allowed to build slowly
> enough to build well, and boldly enough to keep moving. If this is your
> season, save this.

*EXAMPLE FILL — swap for your real week:* "I spent two hours picking a font
for Klypt's onboarding screen instead of writing the one sentence that would
have told me the font didn't matter yet."

**On-screen text:**
```
DIFFERENT FIELD. SAME DRIVE.
Clarity comes from deciding, not decorating.
```

**Storyboard:**
1. **(0–3s) Hook** — Founder shot, contemplative, walking away from the desk.
2. **(3–10s) Struggle** — Cut to the specific moment of avoidance (planning instead of deciding).
3. **(10–24s) Shift** — Voiceover carries the lesson over a calmer visual — path, window, horizon.
4. **(24–32s) Proof** — Founder returning to the desk, sitting back down, decisive posture.
5. **(32–40s) Close** — "Different field. Same drive." end card.

**AI image prompts:**
1. "Editorial photo of a woman in warm, elegant business-casual clothing walking away from a desk toward a sunlit window, contemplative mood, Beyond the Pitch warm palette, natural light, no text."
2. "Wide shot of a wooden path through golden hills at sunset, warm tones, symbolic of a long road ahead, editorial photography, no people, no text."
3. "Editorial photo of a woman sitting back down at a desk with quiet resolve, hand on an open notebook, warm window light, sage and terracotta accents in frame."

**Photo-to-video motion:** Standard day spec; use the slowest push-in of the three posts (start ~102%, end ~106%) — PM posts should feel calmer, not more energetic.

**B-roll:** Hand closing a laptop; a slow walk from desk to window and back; coffee going cold.

**Caption:**
> Most people hide inside planning because choosing a real problem makes the
> idea feel real. Clarity comes from deciding, not decorating. Beyond the
> Pitch is the decisions, habits, and conversations that actually build
> something — not the highlight reel. If this is your season, save this.
> #BeyondThePitch #FounderReality #BuildInPublic

**Platform variations:**
- **IG/FB Reel:** As scripted, slower pacing, no upbeat music — ambient/instrumental.
- **TikTok:** Open directly on the specific avoidance moment (concrete beats abstract on this platform), lesson follows.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Keep captions minimal — this post should feel less text-heavy, more observational.
- **Pinterest Idea Pin:** Skip — this format is founder-mood-driven and underperforms as a static pin; don't force it.
- **Blog expansion angle:** Fold into a monthly "what building actually looked like this month" recap post rather than a standalone article.
